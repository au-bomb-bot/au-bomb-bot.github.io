# Progress This Week

[cite_start]This week, the team received their PDR document back, discussed improvements with the TAs, and implemented those changes[cite: 52]. They also selected a tentative motor for the robotic arm and sent the specifications to Dr. Rose for feedback. [cite_start]Additionally, a time was scheduled to review industrial cameras provided by Dr. Roberts[cite: 53, 54].

# Project Risks and Obsticles

While no new obstacles were determined this week, Dr. Rose brought a significant risk to the team's attention regarding the chosen motor. The motor has very high voltage and torque requirements, which may be excessive for the project's needs. There is also concern that the heat generated by the motor could affect the chamber's temperature requirements.

# Plans for Future

The team's objectives for the next week include evaluating the cameras provided by Dr. Roberts to see if they fit the system's needs and meeting with sponsors on November 19. They also plan to begin simulations to test the current motor choice and complete the first draft of the IDR. Good news was received from the sponsors, who confirmed that the government shutdown was avoided and they remain available for discussion.
