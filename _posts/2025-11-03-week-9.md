# Progress This Week

This week, the team divided up roles for the Interim Design Review (IDR) report and refined the CAD models for the robotic arm. These preparations are essential for the upcoming deadline after Thanksgiving break.

# Project Risks and Obsticles

The team encountered an obstacle regarding the camera system. It was collectively determined that most cameras meeting the safety requirements are too large to comfortably fit on the robotic arm. Consequently, the team decided that mounting cameras to the chamber walls would be the most effective solution. A major risk currently facing the project is the ongoing government shutdown; until it is resolved, the team cannot contact sponsors, which could impact the project if questions arise that only they can answer.

# Plans for Future

The objectives for next week include meeting as a team to begin assembling the IDR report and meeting with Dr. Roberts to touch base on the progress made so far. The team aims to have the bulk of the IDR finished before the Thanksgiving break begins.
