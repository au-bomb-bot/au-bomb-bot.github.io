# Progress This Week

This week, the team successfully finished the Preliminary Design Review (PDR) presentation and presented it to the sponsors on Monday. This marks a significant milestone in validating the initial design concepts.

# Project Risks and Obsticles

During the PDR presentation, an obstacle was identified regarding the robotic arm's movement speed. The team was alerted that the arm needs to make adjustments swiftly because the chamber conditions change rapidly, a factor that had not been previously prioritized. Additionally, a risk was highlighted concerning the design selection process. The team was warned that relying solely on decision matrices might not be thorough enough to account for all variables, which could lead to risky design choices if not supplemented with further analysis.

# Plans for Future

Looking ahead, the team's main objective is to meet with sponsors onsite this Saturday to tour and observe the explosive atmosphere chamber. The team will also continue refining the project designs, specifically incorporating the feedback received during the PDR presentation regarding arm speed and design selection method.
