# Progress This Week

The team successfully visited the site and took specialized measurements of the explosive atmosphere chamber that were not available in the provided documents. This site visit was crucial for verifying physical constraints.

# Project Risks and Obsticles

A new obstacle was discovered during the site visit: the portholes are smaller than anticipated. This reduces the available space for feeding wires into the chamber, requiring the team to be much more careful about the size and quantity of wires used[cite: 98, 99]. Current project risks include durability issues in the event of an ignition, the inability to test the system in a full explosion, and the potential for a government shutdown preventing future site visits.

# Plans for Future

For the upcoming week, the team plans to meet to discuss the next steps and assign responsibilities for the Interim Design Review (IDR). The team currently feels satisfied with the PDR, presentation, and site visit results, and is now shifting focus toward completing the IDR as the last main goal of the semester.
